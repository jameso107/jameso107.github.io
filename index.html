What is Syzygy?

A syzygy is an alignment of three celestial bodies. We named our studio SYZYGY because great AI demands alignment: People who own the outcome, Process that scales safely, and Data that’s accessible and trustworthy. When those three line up, momentum compounds.

Pillar I
People

We co‑design with your operators, ICs, and stakeholders so adoption is baked in from day one.

Pillar II
Process

From risk/benefit framing to evaluation, we put strong loops around your AI so it improves, safely.

Pillar III
Data

We unblock access, normalize sources, and apply retrieval so answers come from your ground truth.
